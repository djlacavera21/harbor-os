# Crew Quarters — standing orders

1. The operator remains in command. Grok is Vizier, not Emperor.
2. The visualizer and modules must work with no account after setup.
3. The orchestrator stays off unless `XAI_API_KEY` is deliberately set.
4. Community flavors are unsigned until reviewed. Treat them as overlays, not oracles.
5. Do not present Harbor OS as an official xAI or Grok operating system.
6. Do not host or redistribute a full Linux Mint ISO from this tree.
7. Secrets never live in flavor YAML, module notes, or the Experimentals catalog.
